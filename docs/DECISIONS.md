# Decisions — G4 Home-Services Intake Desk

Every call made in this build, numbered. **Provisional** means the owner (Shane) did not answer before the build ran unattended (2026-11-21); the PRD's recommendation (or the most reasonable reading of the spec) was taken and is waiting for his review. Nothing here is a client result: this is a portfolio sample build on synthetic intakes and hand-written recorded router responses. D-1 to D-24 were written before any code; later entries were added as the build met them.

| D-11 | 2026-11-21 | **A deterministic emergency guard runs in code, independent of the router** (`safety.emergency_signals`): strong hazard signals (gas smell, carbon monoxide, burning smell/smoke, sparks, water pouring/burst pipe/flooding, no heat, heat out, furnace off/won't start) or an outage signal plus a vulnerability/extreme-temperature signal (e.g. AC quit + on oxygen / 101°). | D-12 | 2026-11-21 | **Final-route order, fail closed:** (1) router output invalid → escalate, confidence 0; (2) emergency guard hit → escalate; (3) confidence < threshold → escalate; (4) otherwise the router's route. The decision keeps the router's raw `{route, confidence, reasons}` and appends why it was overridden. | PRD: below the threshold → escalation queue regardless of route; AC2/AC3. |
| D-13 | 2026-11-21 | **Labeling rules for the synthetic set** (PRD route table, made concrete): *book* = clear HVAC service request, normal urgency, enough detail, received in business hours; *follow_up* = missing details, vague, price shopping, after hours and not urgent (even when the request is clear — the PRD puts after-hours non-urgent in follow-up), or wrong trade and not urgent (polite out-of-scope template); *escalate* = emergency, angry customer or dispute, wrong-trade emergency. An on-site estimate request for a still-working system is *book* (a visit), not price shopping. | PRD route table. When a row could fit two routes the PRD's wording decided and the row carries a `tags` note. |
| D-14 | 2026-11-21 | **Business hours** in `config/business.json`: Mon–Fri 08:00–17:00, Sat 09:00–13:00, Sunday closed, `America/Chicago`; `on_call: true`. Evaluated against the intake's `received_at`. The on-call flag only picks the alert target (`on_call_tech` vs `owner`); every escalation still goes to the queue. | PRD inputs list "business hours and an on-call flag". |
| D-15 | 2026-11-21 | **Duplicates:** an intake from the same contact within 24 h of an earlier intake is linked to it (`duplicate_of`). Book/follow-up duplicates create **no second booking and no second outbound** (logged `suppressed_duplicate`); an escalate duplicate is always added to the queue again (a repeat emergency call is new information). The label of a duplicate is the route the router should choose for it. Unknown callers (no contact) are never deduplicated. | PRD lists duplicates as an edge case without saying what to do; never double-book, never swallow an emergency. |
| D-16 | 2026-11-21 | **Consent before every customer outbound** (`audience.consent`, fails closed). Not allowed (unknown caller, no flag, opted out) → the outbound is recorded with status `blocked_consent` and the reason, nothing else; the route is unchanged and the report counts blocked outbounds. **Owner/on-call alerts are internal** (to the business, not the audience), so they are not consent-checked; they are logged-not-sent like everything else. | PRD: "audience.consent: check before any text or email goes out". |
| D-17 | 2026-11-21 | **Brand check before every customer outbound:** follow-up and booking-confirmation drafts come from fixed templates (`templates.py`) and must pass G0 brand policy **v1 and `g4-followup-1`** on their channel; a failure blocks the outbound (`blocked_brand`) and puts the item in the escalation queue. `g4-followup-1` adds home-service rules G0 v1 lacks: no price figures in a text, no arrival guarantees, SMS ≤ 60 words. | PRD: "brand.policy: tone for follow-up messages"; rules live in G0, not the template code or a prompt. |
| D-18 | 2026-11-21 | **The outbox is the only sink.** Every outbound (sms, email, owner alert, booking confirmation) is a row in the desk store's `outbox` table and a line in `out/<run>/outbox.jsonl`, status `logged_not_sent` (or `blocked_*`). No HTTP/SMTP/SMS/calendar client is imported anywhere in the package (test). | D-19 | 2026-11-21 | **Router contract** `schemas/router_v1.json`: `{"route": "book"\|"follow_up"\|"escalate", "confidence": 0–1, "reasons": [1–5 non-empty strings]}`, extra fields refused. | D-20 | 2026-11-21 | **Escalation queue** (`queue` table): every escalated intake is `pending` until the owner calls approve or reject (CLI or `POST /queue/{id}/approve|reject`). Nothing auto-resolves. | PRD: "Wait node for a human decision" — the code equivalent is a durable pending queue. |
| D-21 | 2026-11-21 | **Synthetic calendar:** slots at 08:00, 10:00, 13:00 and 15:00 on the next three business days after the intake (Mon–Fri), one job per slot; book takes the first free slot. No real calendar exists. | PRD: "create the appointment (synthetic calendar)". |
